About this Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy explains how Service Plaza (“Service Plaza”, “SP”, “we”, “us” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on https://serviceplaza.co.uk (the “Website”). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy at https://serviceplaza.co.uk/privacy-policy.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your computer, phone or other device when you visit a website. Cookies can allow a website to recognise a browser, maintain a secure session, remember settings or understand how the website is used.

Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, scripts and software development kits that store information on, or access information from, a device.

2. Cookies currently used by Service Plaza

Service Plaza currently uses strictly necessary cookies associated with account authentication and security.

These cookies allow Business Users and administrators to sign in securely, maintain an authenticated session, refresh that session where necessary and protect restricted account or administration pages. The Website cannot provide those requested account functions reliably without them.

The authentication cookies are provided through our Supabase authentication service. Their names may contain a project-specific prefix and may change when the underlying authentication service is updated.

Cookie category: Strictly necessary

Purpose: Authentication, session continuity and security for business and administrator accounts.

Typical duration: Session-based or limited-duration persistent cookies required to maintain and refresh a secure sign-in. Exact duration may be controlled by the authentication provider and account session configuration.

Consent: These cookies are necessary to provide sign-in and account functionality requested by the user and cannot be disabled through a Service Plaza preference control.

3. Analytics, advertising and targeting cookies

Service Plaza does not currently use Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Hotjar, behavioural advertising cookies or retargeting cookies on the Website.

If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent before those technologies are set or accessed.

4. Website links and third parties

The Website includes links to third-party websites, including listed businesses’ websites and social-media pages. When you follow a link, the destination website may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Service Plaza does not control those technologies. You should read the destination website’s cookies and privacy information.

Our server may record that a Website visitor selected an outbound business website link so that we can maintain basic operational records. This does not require us to place a non-essential advertising cookie on your device.

5. Managing cookies

You can use your browser settings to view, delete or block cookies. Blocking strictly necessary authentication cookies may prevent you from signing in, remaining signed in, saving an application or using protected account functions.

Browser controls vary by provider and device. Refer to your browser’s help pages for current instructions.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy when the Website, our service providers or our use of cookies changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
